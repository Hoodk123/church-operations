What changed from your test

  • "New visitor" is a normal button in the page header. The full-width bar stays on phones only.
  • A person already checked in shows "Here 09:42" instead of a second Check in button, so nobody is counted twice.
  • "New Convert" leaves the result row. It moves into the person's drawer as "Mark as New Convert", with a confirmation, because it is rare and should not sit next to Check in.
  • "Came last Sunday, not here yet" is a quick list for regulars, one tap each. It is built from the attendance table.
  • The Today panel replaces the separate Today page. "Undo" removes a mistaken check-in (Admin and Info Desk).

Rules for this page

  • Order: asked for pastor, then newly assigned, then never contacted, then longest since contact.
  • "Log" has a "Finished with this person" switch. When on, the status becomes Completed, the person leaves the list, and the log keeps the record.
  • After any log, the card moves to "Done today" at once, so nobody calls the same person twice.
  • The panel message is a template with {name} filled in. A volunteer picks one template; the admin writes and edits them.

Fix · Follow-up Board

Dragging a card

In your screenshots the card is cut off at the column edge and a small scrollbar appears inside the column, which is what makes everything shake. The card has to float above the board while you hold it, and the columns must not scroll on their own.

The card follows the pointer

The dragged card is drawn in a floating layer above everything, with a stronger shadow. The original spot shows a dashed placeholder, and the column under the pointer gets an outline.

One scrollbar only

Columns grow with their cards. The board scrolls sideways in one place, and the page scrolls up and down. No column has its own scrollbar, so nothing resizes while you drag.

A click is not a drag

A card must move a few pixels before a drag starts, so tapping still opens the person's drawer. On phones, hold briefly or use the ... menu.
