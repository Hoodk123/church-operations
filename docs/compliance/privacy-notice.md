When you visit a service or start a class with us, our info desk volunteers record a few details on the church's own system and follow you up with care.

**What we record.** Your name, phone number (and email if you give it), your area, your age group, how you found our church, what you'd like contact about (WhatsApp, SMS, call, email), and — as you go through them — your class (M1) and baptism progress, and notes of our visits or calls.

**Why.** To welcome you, pray for you if you ask, invite you to classes, and remember you next Sunday.

**Who sees it.** Only our staff team: the follow-up volunteers, the info desk, and the pastor. Your private notes and answers about counseling or personal matters are seen only by the pastor and administrator. We never post your details anywhere public.

**A note on faith information.** Because ours is a church, our records include baptism and class progress — faith information. Strong data protection laws (such as ours — see below) count this as sensitive; we therefore keep it on church computers only.

**Where it lives.** On a computer kept at the church, on the church's own network. It does not go to any outside company's cloud. When we open a WhatsApp or SMS message for you, the message passes through that app (or your phone carrier) — beyond our control; we never store the message.

**Your rights.** You may ask to see what we hold about you, correct it, get a copy, or ask us to delete you. Just tell any staff member or write to the contact below, and we will respond within a reasonable time.
