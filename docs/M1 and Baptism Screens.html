M1 Class and Baptism Screens

How the M1 class (three days) and baptism would work inside the system you already have. People who need M1 and people who need baptism sit in separate lists, each on its own page. Names, numbers and dates below are examples.

For the presentation

What works today, and what this adds

Working now
  • Register people with category, M1 status, HBF group and baptism status
  • Sort, filter and group the people list
  • Check-in, follow-ups with call and WhatsApp, and the follow-up board (being polished)
  • Roles: Admin, Info Desk, Follow-up Coordinator, Pastor
Next, shown on this page
  • An M1 Class page with Day 1, 2, 3 attendance ticks
  • Register people who show up on class day
  • Reminders before class and catch-up messages after a missed day
  • A reply link so absent people can tell us why
  • Shared study materials, certificates, and a Baptism page
The journey

From "needs M1" to certificate and baptism

Needs M1→Enrolled→Day 1→Day 2→Day 3→Completed→Certificate→Needs baptism→Baptism date→Baptized
Missed a day→Catch-up message with notes and link→They tell us why→Back in class

Why a link and not a form in the app

  • The person is not a team member, so they cannot log in. The link is the key, and each one belongs to one person and one day.
  • The page shows only their first name, what was studied, and the next class date.
  • If someone prefers a phone call, the volunteer records the reason by hand from the Missed tab.
